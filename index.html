F-commerce scale signal

BSS reported estimates of 500,000–1 million entrepreneur-focused Facebook pages, with 200,000–250,000 active Facebook-commerce entrepreneurs. Treat these as industry estimates, not a verified census.

This supports a broad prospect pool, but individual lead quality still needs manual verification.

Strategy: start with apparel and adjacent visual-product niches, then test skincare, custom gifts, home décor, and jewelry. Target sellers with observable order volume and operational friction—not simply every page without a website.

Google prospecting toolkit

Search operators help discover prospects; they do not verify activity, ownership, or contact accuracy.

Choose a niche card to prefill its first keyword. Try city-specific variants: Dhaka, Chattogram/Chittagong, Sylhet, Rajshahi, Khulna, Gazipur, Narayanganj, Cumilla, Bogura.

Useful operator patterns

  • site:facebook.com "Niche" "Bangladesh" — discover public Facebook pages/posts.
  • site:facebook.com "Niche" "WhatsApp" — find sellers publishing WhatsApp contact.
  • "Niche" "Bangladesh" ("contact" OR "about us") -facebook.com — independent sites.
  • site:instagram.com "Niche" "Bangladesh" — Instagram business profiles.
  • "Niche" "wholesale" "Bangladesh" — wholesale / distributor leads.

Public business email discovery

Search for public business contact pages or email text published for customer inquiries. Try multiple domains and spelling variants, then confirm the email on the official page before adding it to CRM.

  • Use public business contact details only.
  • Do not assume an email in a search snippet belongs to the business.
  • Respect platform rules, privacy expectations, and opt-outs.

Minimum qualification checklist

  • Active page / recent posts and visible customer activity.
  • Products/SKUs or variants create meaningful discovery complexity.
  • Inbox, WhatsApp, or comments are a major ordering channel.
  • Website missing, broken, slow, confusing, or disconnected from order handling.
  • At least one specific, verifiable pain point to mention.
  • Public business contact channel and correct decision-maker path.

Go-to-market sequence

Build depth in one segment before expanding.

First 500 leads

Start with T-shirt/clothing, beauty/skincare, customized gifts, home décor, and jewelry. Research each lead and prioritize quality over raw volume.

Next 500 leads

Expand to boutique/women’s fashion, cosmetics, perfume, saree/sharee, and Panjabi/kurta.

Expand to sectors 11–30

Test streetwear, kids/baby, bags, footwear, home accessories, furniture, haircare, food brands, coffee/tea, bakery/frozen food, kitchen products, and educational toys.

Test sectors 31–50

Expand into sports, gadgets, smart home, pets, Islamic products, books, stationery, plants, crafts, travel, cleaning, and utility products.

Weekly review

Track contact-to-reply, reply-to-meeting, meeting-to-proposal, and proposal-to-win by niche and message angle. Keep investing in segments that show actual conversion.

Core positioning to test

“We help Facebook-first product businesses organize their product catalogue, answer common customer questions faster, and connect inquiries to order workflows—with a website and AI support where it genuinely fits.”

Avoid promising guaranteed sales growth. Sell a diagnosed workflow improvement and confirm feasibility, data access, integrations, and human handoff before proposing scope.
